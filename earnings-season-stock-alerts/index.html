Earnings season: the stock alerts to set before the report

Earnings season is when a stock you've held quietly for months can move 8% in one session. Third-quarter reports start in mid-October, led by the big banks, and run through early November. The fix is simple: set your alerts before the report, so the reaction comes to you.

1. Know which of your stocks report soon

Go through your watchlist and note each company's report date and whether it reports before the open or after the close. Your broker or any finance site lists them.

2. Set a percent-move alert sized to what matters to you

For each stock, pick the size of move that would make you do something, say 5% up or down from the prior close, and set a percent-change alert. If it fires, read why. If it doesn't, you didn't spend the evening refreshing a quote. Triggerly's Percentage Change alert can measure from the open, the prior close, or any reference price.

3. Add price alerts at your decision levels

If there's a price where you'd add to the position or cut it, set a price alert there too.

4. Watch volume the day after

The day after a report, heavy volume tells you whether the move has real participation. A volume spike alert at 3x the 20-day average is a good filter.

A note on timing

Many companies report after the close or before the open. Triggerly tracks regular-session prices, so for those reports your alert fires once regular trading opens and the move shows up in the session price. That's usually the moment you can act anyway.

Quick earnings checklist
  • Report date and time noted for each holding
  • Percent-move alert (for example ±5% from prior close)
  • Price alerts at your add and exit levels
  • Volume spike alert for the day after

FAQ

When does Q3 2026 earnings season start?

Third-quarter reports typically begin in mid-October with the large US banks and continue through early November.

What percent-move alert should I use for earnings?

Use the size of move that would change what you do. 5% is a common starting point; volatile stocks may need 8 to 10%.

Will Triggerly alert me on after-hours earnings moves?

No. Triggerly tracks regular-session prices, so after-hours moves show up when regular trading opens.
