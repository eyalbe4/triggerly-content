About Triggerly

Triggerly is a free Android stock alert app. Track 5 stocks free with as many alerts on each as you like; each friend who installs through your invite link and signs up adds 1 more stock, up to 15. Set the rules that match how you trade, from a simple price target to 14-day RSI, MACD or trailing stops, and get notified by push, email or Telegram. Those channels are set once for the whole account. Alerts fire during regular US market hours and are checked every 5 minutes; the app also shows pre-market and after-hours prices. When an alert fires, open Alert Analysis for what likely caused the move. Tap any stock for analyst price targets and the latest news. No subscriptions, no ads, no in-app purchases.
