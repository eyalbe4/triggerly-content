# triggerly-content

Public content for [Triggerly](https://triggerly-app.com/), the free Android stock alert app ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.triggerly.android&utm_source=guides&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=levelping_202610&utm_content=github-readme)).

Track 5 stocks free with as many alerts on each as you like; each friend who installs through your invite link and signs up adds 1 more stock, up to 15. Alerts fire during regular US market hours and are checked every 5 minutes. The app also shows pre-market and after-hours prices. Push, email, and Telegram are account-wide notification settings: open @triggerly_app_bot, tap Start, and paste the Chat ID into Settings.

SMA and EMA alerts watch price versus an average, or an average versus a price you set. They do not alert when two averages cross. RSI uses a fixed 14-day period on daily closes. Percent-change alerts compare with the prior close, the price when the alert was set, the opening gap (today's open versus the prior close, checked at 9:35 ET), or the daily close.
