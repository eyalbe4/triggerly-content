How to get stock alerts in Telegram, for free

Push notifications get buried with everything else on your phone. Sending stock alerts to Telegram puts them in one searchable chat that shows up on your phone, tablet and desktop at the same time. Here's why it works well and how to set it up for free.

Why Telegram is good for stock alerts

  • Every device. Telegram syncs across phone and desktop, so you see the alert on whatever screen you're using.
  • A searchable history. Every alert stays in the chat. Want to know when a stock last crossed $200? Search it.
  • Its own notification settings. Give the alerts chat a distinct sound or let it break through when other chats are muted.
  • Separate from noise. Alerts don't compete with dozens of other app notifications.

Set it up with Triggerly

  1. Install Triggerly on Android (free, no ads).
  2. Turn on Telegram delivery in the app and follow the steps it shows to link your Telegram account.
  3. Create an alert: price target, percent move, volume spike, moving-average cross, RSI, MACD, or a combination.
  4. Choose Telegram as the delivery channel, alone or together with push and email.

Tips

  • Pin the alerts chat so it stays at the top.
  • Use two channels for the important ones. Telegram plus push for the alerts you can't miss; Telegram only for informational ones.
  • Keep the list tidy. If an alert fires often and you ignore it, raise the threshold or delete it.

What about email?

Triggerly can also email you, and the email links straight into the full Alert Analysis. Email is good for a record of alerts you review in the evening; Telegram is better for things you want to see right away.
